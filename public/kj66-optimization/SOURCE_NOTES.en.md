## How evidence is used

**Direct KJ66 research:** Xiang and colleagues provide the compressor results used in the charts. Ling and colleagues investigated a modified compressor stage. Neither establishes a performance value for the supplied CAD reconstruction.

**KJ66-derived studies:** Capata and Achille discuss a larger power-generating concept. Wang and Luo investigate a KJ66-based combustor–turbine configuration. Differences from the current reconstruction must remain explicit.

**Related engines and general methods:** the clearance study, SR-30 optimization work, BMT120 matching work and dynamic-clearance method support research approaches and trade-offs. Their dimensions, gains and limits are not transferable specifications.

**General definitions and manufacturer context:** NASA supplies the performance definitions. JetCat supplies an example of system integration, not an independently measured retrofit comparison.

**Article proposals:** the development order, parameter studies, installed-mass boundary, acceptance criteria and hypothetical 10% targets are proposals for this project. They are not quotations, literature predictions or completed results.

## Important interpretation limits

| Reference | Limitation preserved in the article |
|---|---|
| Xiang, Schlüter and Duan | Numerical speed-line maxima, not full digitized maps, equal-flow points or whole-engine efficiency. |
| Ling, Wong and Armfield | Impeller and diffuser were modified together; this is not a measured retained-rotor retrofit gain. |
| Capata and Achille | Different, larger concept; full combustor simulation was not completed in that study. |
| Wang and Luo | Numerical interaction study on their KJ66-based configuration, not a test of this project. |
| van Eck and colleagues | Different compressor stages; operating-margin trade-offs prevent a universal smallest-gap rule. |
| Dynamic-clearance study | General geometric modelling relationship; no KJ66-specific approved gap follows from it. |
| Oppong and colleagues | Related BMT120 engine; used for the matching lesson, not a KJ66 output prediction. |
| Guo and colleagues | Compressor pressure-ratio result on SR-30, not an engine thrust percentage. |
| Deng and colleagues | Whole-engine modelling precedent checked against reference data, not a model developed for this blog. |
| JetCat | Manufacturer description of integration, not proof of a particular KJ66 mass saving. |
